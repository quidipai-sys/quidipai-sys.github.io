Official Application

Duflot Stopwatch: Lap Timer

High-precision digital stopwatch, interval lap timer, and training history tracker designed for Android.

About the Application

Duflot Stopwatch: Lap Timer is a clean, lightweight, and modern utility built for athletes, trainers, runners, students, and everyday productivity. It offers millisecond-accurate timing, lap split calculations, customizable countdown presets, and offline session history.

  • No Advertising: Enjoy a completely distraction-free experience with zero ads.
  • 100% Offline: Operates entirely on your local device without requiring an active internet connection.
  • Zero Dangerous Permissions: Does not request access to your camera, microphone, storage, contacts, or location.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Duflot Stopwatch: Lap Timer developed and published by DUFLOT FRANCK.

1. Information Collection and Use

We respect your privacy. Duflot Stopwatch: Lap Timer does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII). All stopwatch data, lap times, countdown durations, and session logs remain strictly stored on your local device memory.

2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The application does NOT integrate any third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or data brokers. It operates independently as a standalone offline utility.

3. Device Permissions

The app only uses normal, non-dangerous system hardware capabilities required for its core functionality:

  • Vibration (Haptic Feedback): Provides tactile response when pressing buttons and alerts when a timer finishes.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for general audiences of all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

5. GDPR & Compliance

In full compliance with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google Play Developer Program Policies, no user tracking or profiling occurs.
