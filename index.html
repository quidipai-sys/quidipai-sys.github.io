Duflot Stopwatch: Lap Timer

High-precision digital stopwatch, interval lap timer, and training history tracker designed for Android.

About the Application

Duflot Stopwatch: Lap Timer is a clean, lightweight, and modern utility developed by DUFLOT FRANCK. Built for athletes, trainers, runners, students, and everyday productivity, it provides millisecond-accurate timing, lap split calculations, customizable countdown presets, and offline session history.

  • No Advertising: Distraction-free experience with zero ads.
  • 100% Offline: Operates entirely on your device without requiring an internet connection.
  • Zero Dangerous Permissions: Does not request access to camera, microphone, storage, contacts, or location.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Duflot Stopwatch: Lap Timer published by DUFLOT FRANCK.

1. Information Collection and Use

We respect your privacy. Duflot Stopwatch: Lap Timer does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or personally identifiable data (PII). All stopwatch data, lap times, countdown durations, and session logs remain strictly stored on your local device memory.

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The application does NOT integrate any third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or data brokers. It operates independently as a standalone offline utility.

3. Device Permissions

The app only uses normal, non-dangerous system hardware capabilities required for its core functionality:

  • Vibration (Haptic Feedback): Provides tactile response when pressing buttons and alerts when a timer finishes.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for general audiences of all ages and does not collect any data from children.
